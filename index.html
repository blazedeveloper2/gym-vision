Gym Technique Helper

Live body tracking with push-up and squat form feedback. Runs entirely on your device.

  • Tracker draws your skeleton and shows live elbow, shoulder, hip and knee angles.
  • Push-ups counts reps, measures how deep you go and flags sagging or piking hips.
  • Squats counts reps, checks you reach parallel and flags forward lean or caving knees.
  • Video mode analyzes a clip you recorded, so it also works on a PC with no camera.
Add to Home Screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Video never leaves your device. Open source ·
